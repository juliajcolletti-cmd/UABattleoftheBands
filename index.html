Where Your Money Goes

The Joe Espy
SGA Scholarship

Every dollar raised at Battle of the Bands goes directly toward supporting University of Alabama students navigating unexpected financial hardship. This event welcomes the entire UA community and greater Tuscaloosa area — students, student organizations, and local businesses, to come together for a cause that makes a real difference.

About the Fund
What Is It?

The Joe Espy SGA Scholarship Fund is a need-based emergency scholarship administered by the University of Alabama's Student Government Association. It was created to provide timely financial relief to students facing sudden, unexpected hardship that often arrives without warning.

Unlike traditional merit-based scholarships, the Espy Fund is utility-based. That means it helps students cover real, immediate needs: rent, car repairs, utility bills, and other essentials that fall outside of standard financial aid. Applications are reviewed individually, and assistance is provided as quickly as possible.

The fund is open to all enrolled UA students — full-time and part-time, throughout the academic term.

Who It Helps
Who Can Apply?

Any enrolled UA student facing a documented financial emergency is eligible to apply. Qualifying circumstances include sudden loss of household income, unexpected medical costs, housing or food insecurity, natural disasters, the death of an immediate family member, or any other unforeseen crisis that places a student in financial distress.

Every application is reviewed on a case-by-case basis by the SGA committee, with the goal of connecting students with the support they need without delay.

Why It Matters
Your Impact

Behind every dollar raised at Battle of the Bands is a student who simply needed a bridge. Not a handout. A bridge. Something to carry them from an unexpected moment back to the path they were already on.
